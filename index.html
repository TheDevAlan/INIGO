MOVE
Research suggests taking a brief active break from sitting every 30 to 60 minutes. Moving regularly is essential for maintaining an active metabolism, boosting blood flow, and easing pressure on the spine.

DRINK
Scientific guidelines recommend drinking about one glass of water every hour during your workday, aiming for a total of 2 to 3 liters daily. Consistent hydration is essential for maintaining focus, preventing fatigue, and supporting your metabolism while sitting at a desk. Keeping a water bottle in plain sight serves as an excellent visual reminder to take regular sips.
DRINK
BREATHE
Practice the "perfect breath" by inhaling and exhaling for 5.5 seconds each through your nose, with your tongue resting gently on the roof of your mouth. Cultivating this mindful awareness of your breath, especially at the end of a break, is highly recommended to center your focus. It acts as a powerful mental reset, ensuring you return to your next productive work phase fully energized and ready.
