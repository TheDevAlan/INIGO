# Privacy Policy for INI:GO

*Last updated: 2026-07-16*

INI:GO ("the app") is a personal productivity timer and daily wellbeing tracker for Windows. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

INI:GO does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics or tracking of any kind built into the app.

## Data storage

All data you enter — focus session history, daily check-ins (mood, steps, sleep, workouts, gratitude notes), custom trackers, reminders, and blocked-site lists — is stored **only on your own device**, in a local file under your Windows user profile (`%APPDATA%\INIGO`). This data never leaves your computer. INI:GO has no server, and the app cannot read or transmit this file anywhere.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete this folder; you can remove it yourself at any time to erase all app data.

## Notifications

INI:GO uses the Windows notification system to remind you about focus sessions and breaks. These notifications are generated and shown entirely on your device — no notification content is sent to any external service.

## Third-party network requests

The app's interface loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`) at runtime, as is standard practice for web-based typography. This means your device makes a request to Google's servers to fetch font files, which may expose your IP address to Google in the same way any font-loading webpage would. No other data is sent as part of this request. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how Google handles this.

## External links

INI:GO contains a couple of optional links (e.g., "Report a bug" and "Buy me a coffee") that open in your default web browser **only if you click them**. These take you to GitHub (github.com) or Buy Me a Coffee (buymeacoffee.com) respectively. INI:GO does not send any of your app data to these sites — clicking them is a normal outbound link, governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

INI:GO does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (e.g., if a feature adds cloud sync), this document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
